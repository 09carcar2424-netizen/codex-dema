# Tax and Withholding Estimate v1

This document is an operating guide, not tax advice.

BOSS should show customers clear estimates and explanations, but final classification must be confirmed by a tax professional.

## Why This Exists

Older or non-technical customers may misunderstand:

- AdSense revenue
- BOSS operation fees
- referral rewards
- withholding tax
- payout timing

The portal should explain these clearly before disputes happen.

## Recommended Portal Sections

Customer view:

- My AdSense revenue: paid directly to customer by Google
- BOSS operation fee: amount customer owes BOSS
- Referral reward: amount BOSS may pay customer
- Estimated withholding: reference only
- Net expected payout: reference only
- Tax notice: final amount may differ

Admin view:

- customer tax profile
- business registration status
- withholding category
- settlement month
- gross amount
- estimated withholding
- net payable
- payout status
- memo and dispute notes

## Default Categories

### Business Income 3.3 Reference

Use for many personal-service style payments when tax professional confirms it is appropriate.

Reference estimate:

- income tax: 3%
- local income tax: 0.3%
- total: 3.3%

### Other Income 8.8 Reference

Use only as a reference scenario when tax professional says 기타소득 treatment is appropriate.

Reference estimate:

- effective total often shown as 8.8% when 60% necessary expense and local income tax are considered
- exact treatment depends on payment type and law

### Invoice Required

Use when the recipient is a business or corporation issuing invoice/tax invoice.

### Needs Review

Default for new customers and referral rewards until tax review.

## Risk Controls

- Do not present estimates as final tax advice.
- Show tax disclaimer beside every estimate.
- Store customer confirmation logs.
- Keep settlement and reward records immutable after confirmation.
- Allow admin adjustment with memo.
- Require tax profile before payout.
